# Versions

The version number is shown at the very bottom of the page, so it's easy to check whether a
device has the latest site. Versions before v1.3 were numbered afterwards, by grouping the
commit history.

## v1.3
- The version number is shown at the bottom of the page.
- A leftover `#...` at the end of the address (from old bookmarks) is removed on load.

## v1.2 (`7346907` to `106a61b`)
- Olympian Mode in pure black and white: white page, an unshaded wheel, and selections and
  buttons in white on black.
- The search bar hides once any word is selected, with "Clear selection" in its place.
- The page no longer bounces on iOS Safari while the wheel turns.
- On touch, the corners around the wheel scroll the page.
- The search clear button is centered.
- "+ Add a secondary emotion" above the wheel while the secondary-emotion question is open.

## v1.1 (`fe34379` to `d543382`)
- Search suggestions that match anywhere in a word and show where each sits on the wheel.
- "Clear selection" above the wheel.
- The opening check is skipped for 15 minutes after Continue, with a link to show it again.
- Smooth layout transitions.

## v1.0 (`0957906` to `7ea050a`)
- Site icon and home-screen app.

## v0.5 (`a183804` to `243a57d`)

  what it wants right now", "What's left?" and Start over.
- Suggestions on the opening check.

## v0.4 (`1c13acf` to `86c7186`)

  locking after an outer-ring selection.

## v0.3 (`cf7136e`)
