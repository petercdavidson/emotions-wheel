    About this practice

    Each emotion on the wheel is asking for something:

      If you can't give the emotion what it wants, what's left? Is there a secondary emotion you're feeling that you can tend to as well?

      If you're still stuck, write it down and bring it to your therapist.

      Tired, hungry, thirsty, triggered: these needs have to be met first, if possible.

      Complete the whole thought: I'm feeling this. This is connected to this. My body needs this. And this is what I'm going to do about it.

      Say it out loud, into a mirror if you can.

      This is a life tool, not a crisis tool. Practice it every day.

      Nothing you type here is saved or sent anywhere. Closing or reloading the page clears it.

      Not a crisis tool. In a crisis in the US, call or text 988.
